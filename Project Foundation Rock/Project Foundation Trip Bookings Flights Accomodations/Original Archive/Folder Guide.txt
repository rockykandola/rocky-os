Inside each trip folder:

Flights contains flight confirmations, itineraries, and booking screenshots.
Accommodations contains hotel, Airbnb, apartment, and voucher files.
Legal and Visas contains eVisas, visa support documents, arrival card records, and passport related screenshots.
Receipts and Insurance contains payment records, travel insurance, and invoice documents.
Other contains useful travel items that do not fit the above categories.

Original Archive contains the original zip backup.
