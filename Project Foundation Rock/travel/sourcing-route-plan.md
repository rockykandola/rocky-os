# Hair Sourcing Route Plan — Flexible Working Version

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Working sequence

1. Chiang Mai base after arrival on Sep 2.
2. Begin vendor-search movement around Sep 15, Sep 18, or Sep 19 depending on supplier readiness and safety research.
3. Myanmar/Burma, Cambodia, and Vietnam in the order that produces the safest and most credible supplier meetings. Do not force a country because it appears on the list.
4. Complete the vendor-search loop approximately Oct 1, Oct 3, or Oct 5, with shorter duration acceptable.
5. India after the lighter experimental markets, with Sanchit Dandeli and the major inventory purchase as the commercial priority.
6. Return to USA after India; flight and baggage plan remain open.

## Planning ranges

- Start window: 2026-09-15 through 2026-09-19.
- End window: 2026-10-01 through 2026-10-05.
- Modeled maximum: 20 days from Sep 15 through Oct 5.
- Preferred operating principle: book only the next irreversible leg once the supplier, safety, visa, baggage, and exit-plan evidence supports it.

## Required before each country

- Current government travel advisory and entry rules.
- Visa and border/airline feasibility.
- Supplier contacts and meeting confirmations.
- Airport-to-sourcing-region route.
- Local transport and payment plan.
- Test purchase ceiling and true landed-cost worksheet.
- Exit plan if the opportunity or safety picture is poor.
